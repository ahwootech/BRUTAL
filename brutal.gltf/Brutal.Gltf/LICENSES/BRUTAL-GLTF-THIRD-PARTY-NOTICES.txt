RocketWerkz.Brutal.Gltf

This package contains no third-party library. Its glTF type definitions are generated
from The Khronos Group's glTF 2.0 JSON schemas, and the descriptive text of those
schemas is reproduced as the API documentation in Brutal.Gltf.xml. The license Khronos
applies to those schemas is reproduced in full below.
