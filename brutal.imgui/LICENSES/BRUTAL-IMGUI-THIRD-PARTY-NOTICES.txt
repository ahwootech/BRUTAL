  Dear ImGui vendors three of Sean Barrett's stb libraries, and all three are
  compiled into the Dear ImGui binaries shipped here. They have their own section
  below.

  The managed assemblies are in scope as well as the native libraries. Type names,
  enumerant values and entry-point signatures are generated from the corresponding
  upstream headers, and the backend and demo assemblies are C# translations of
  upstream sources.

  RocketWerkz.Brutal.ImGui.FreeType binds Dear ImGui's FreeType font adapter, which
  is Dear ImGui code and is covered by the Dear ImGui section below. FreeType itself
  is in none of these eight packages; it is supplied by RocketWerkz.Brutal.FreeType,
  whose own notices reproduce the FreeType License in full.
