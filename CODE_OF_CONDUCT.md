# Code of Conduct

BRUTAL follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), together with the [Ahwoo Community Guidelines](https://ahwoo.com/help-centre/community-guidelines). Where they overlap, the stricter rule applies.

## In short

- Be respectful. Criticise code and decisions, not people.
- **Don't direct abuse at maintainers or staff.** Disagreement is fine, but harassment isn't, and it will get you removed even if your technical point is correct.
- No harassment, hate speech, sexual content, doxxing, threats, scams, malware or spam.
- Stay on topic. GitHub issues and PRs are for BRUTAL development. Use the [forums](https://forums.ahwoo.dev/forums/brutal/) for everything else.

## Scope

This applies to the BRUTAL GitHub repositories, the BRUTAL forums, and BRUTAL channels on Ahwoo's Discord, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **conduct@ahwoo.com** (or privately to a moderator). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, close issues and PRs, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code. We follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. Serious breaches (threats, doxxing, sexual content involving minors, malware) skip straight to a permanent ban and may be reported to the platform and to authorities.
