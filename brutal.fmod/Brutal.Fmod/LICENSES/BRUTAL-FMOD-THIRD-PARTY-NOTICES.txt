  win-x64      fmod.dll, fmodL.dll, fmodstudio.dll, fmodstudioL.dll

  osx-arm64    libfmod.dylib, libfmodL.dylib, libfmodstudio.dylib,

above. The osx-arm64 dylibs are universal binaries (arm64 + x86_64). No other
platform is included.
